The decision that matters

Which framework, and when?

Reach for LangChain

Chains, agents, tool-calling, output parsing. Basically the flexible orchestration and generation side of things, Notebook 01's territory.

Reach for LlamaIndex

Multi-format loading, sentence-window, small-to-big. Data ingestion and the harder chunking and retrieval work.

For the trainer

Give them the honest answer: a lot of production stacks just use both. LlamaIndex for ingestion and retrieval, LangChain or LangGraph for orchestration on top of it. Nobody's picking one forever here.

Anticipate these questions

FAQ, what usually comes up here

"Do I need to learn both frameworks?"
Not to a deep level in both, no. Know LlamaIndex's ingestion strengths and LangChain's orchestration strengths well enough to grab the right tool for the job. That's basically what real teams do anyway.
"Which one should I use in my capstone project?"
Whichever one you can extend fastest under time pressure. Both cover what we taught in Notebooks 02 and 03 just fine. Staying consistent in your own codebase matters a lot more than picking the "better" one.
"Is AutoMergingRetriever always better than a fixed sentence-window?"
No. It's more setup, multiple chunk sizes, a docstore, a storage context, and the payoff really shows on long structured documents. For short FAQ-style content, plain sentence-window is usually enough on its own.
