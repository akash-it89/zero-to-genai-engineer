The interview trap

Same loop. Different critic.

Reflection, Reflexion and CRITIC draw the identical cycle. The only difference is what feeds the critic — the orange box.

Reflection
Madaan et al. 2023 · Self-Refine
The model is its own only judge. Cheap, and it will happily approve its own mistake.
Reflexion
Shinn et al. 2023
It saw the code actually fail, and it keeps a written memory across attempts. HumanEval 80 → 91%.
CRITIC
Gou et al. ICLR 2024
Ground truth the model cannot argue with. 2+2 is 4 because the calculator said so.
Say it in one breath: "Reflection judges itself. Reflexion judges what happened, and remembers. CRITIC lets a tool judge."
