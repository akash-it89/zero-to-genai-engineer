Recap

Four questions you should answer out loud

What are the four parts?
Pause, persist, show a payload, resume with an answer.
Why the same thread_id?
It's the key to the saved whiteboard holding the unfinished review.
Why doesn't weather pause?
The router checks the tool name. Only names in RISKY divert.
What does "no" do?
Continues inside human_approval. No restart, no crash.
Why not input()?
It dies with the process. A pause must survive a deploy.
